Dear Raisha,

Happy birthday. I hope this next year has room for all the things you love: tulips and lilies, blue skies and beach sunsets, books, long tea conversations, jhumkas and bangles, a red and black saree, chocolate ice cream, and all the other things I haven't managed to fit on this page.

I know I can overthink and let it spill into our conversations. I’m sorry for the times that has made things feel heavy. I’m working on pausing, listening, and giving you room; it’s mine to work on, not yours to fix.

And I meant what I said: I never want the things I do for you to become a tally or something I hold over you. I want my care to feel like care, and I’ll keep trying to show that in how I act.

I still think Allah brought our paths together. I make dua that, when the time is right, we get to meet again. For today, I just hope you feel celebrated and get to enjoy your day exactly how you want.

Happy birthday, Raisha.
I love you.
